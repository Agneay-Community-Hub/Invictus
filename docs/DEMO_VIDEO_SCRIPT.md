# 30-second demo video: script and shot list

**The idea:** open with Echo, show one machine told in several ways, prove it's real and accurate, and end on the kids' side (play, 3D, classroom). Storytelling first, technology second.

**How to record**
- Use any screen recorder. On Windows, press `Win + Alt + R` with Xbox Game Bar, or use the Snipping Tool's video mode.
- Open the live site in a full-screen browser at 1920×1080. Turn the sound up: Echo's voice is part of shot 1.
- Record each shot separately, then join them in Clipchamp (free on Windows).
- Read the narration slowly. It's about 70 words and fits in 30 seconds.

| Time | What to show (on screen) | Narration (voice-over) |
|---|---|---|
| 0–5 s | Home page → press **Watch Echo's 5-minute story** → **Start the journey**. Let Echo say its first line over the Earthrise photo. Keep Echo's own voice at low volume under yours. | "Since 1958, NASA has left machines all over the solar system. Meet Echo, the last message they ever sent home." |
| 5–9 s | Echo's journey → press → until the light-speed card "Mars · 3 to 22 light-minutes away", then the Spirit and Opportunity scene with its fact card "90 days → 14 years". | "Echo takes kids from the Moon to interstellar space in five minutes…" |
| 9–14 s | Home page → **Ghost Map** → click Opportunity → the dossier opens. Press the full-screen button. Scroll to **This story, three ways**. | "…then every machine tells its own story…" |
| 14–18 s | Click **Watch its cartoon short**: 3 seconds of Opportunity's cartoon with subtitles. Quick cut to its comic. | "…as a cartoon, a comic and a podcast, all 49 of them." |
| 18–22 s | Dossier → **How it worked** → the sky crane animation, steps 3–5. | "Animations show how they really worked…" |
| 22–25 s | 3D Worlds → **Walk on the Moon** at Tranquility Base, turning around the lunar module. | "…and kids can walk the real landing sites." |
| 25–28 s | Home page → **Play** section → **Land it yourself** on Mars: cut the parachute, touch down. Then a 1-second flash of **Check the facts** opened in a dossier, showing the NSSDCA link. | "Every game uses real physics, and every fact is checked against NASA records." |
| 28–30 s | Home page title with the site address. | "Ghosts of Exploration: abandoned, but not forgotten." |

**Tips**
- **Echo:** to jump straight to a scene, open `story.html#5` (Spirit and Opportunity) and press Start. Press **M** to mute Echo if you'd rather use only your own voice.
- **Landing game:** practise the Mars level once so the landing works on camera. Cut the chute at about 500 m, then hold 🔥 when the falling speed goes above about 25 m/s.
- **Captions:** add them in Clipchamp (Text), so the video works with the sound off.
- **Phone shot (optional):** if you have a spare second, show the site on a phone. It's a strong signal for "works anywhere".
- **Slides instead:** Space Apps also accepts a slide deck. `pitch.html` on the live site has 12 slides (press → to advance), and the same deck is in `docs/pitch-deck.pptx` and `docs/pitch-deck.pdf`.

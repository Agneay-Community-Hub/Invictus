A podcast for kids

Ghost Logs: the podcast

Machines in space tell their own stories. In the seasons, each episode is narrated by one instrument, about the one thing only it could do. In Ghost Log Radio, every machine in the archive gets an episode of its own.

This browser can't speak the episodes aloud. You can still read along below.

Episodes

    About the voices

    Each episode is read aloud by your device's built-in voices, so it sounds a bit robotic and changes from device to device. The scripts are ready to be recorded by real people, and those recordings will replace the robot voices.

    For teachers

    Every transcript prints as a reading script. Facts and dates come from NASA mission pages (read in 2026) and the sourced dossiers on this site. The flight team's lines are made up, not quotes from real people.

The Moon tonight

This is the Moon as it really looks today, made from NASA's map of its surface. Every dot is a machine we left there. Gold dots are in daytime right now, blue dots are in the long lunar night.

·

No telescope on Earth can see the machines themselves: even the biggest Apollo leftovers are far too small. But now you know exactly where to point.

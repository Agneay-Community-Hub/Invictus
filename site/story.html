Echo's journey

Meet Echo, the last radio message a spacecraft ever sends home. Travel at the speed of light from the Moon to interstellar space and meet the machines NASA left behind, in about 5 minutes.

Echo talks out loud. Captions are always on, so it works with the sound off too. Every fact comes from NASA mission records.

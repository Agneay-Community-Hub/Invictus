A card game

Ghost Cards

Every machine is a card with four stats. Pick the stat you think beats the computer's card. Win the round and you take both cards.

How to play

Veteran: years since launch. Stamina: time from launch to its last contact. Reach: roughly how far it is from Earth. Discovery: how many big discoveries it made. The higher number wins. A tie puts both cards in the middle, and the next winner takes them too. Whoever wins a round picks the next stat.
